2. Purpose and audience
BusMakro is a public transport app for Athens, Greece, for residents and visitors who ride OASA buses and trolleys, the metro and the tram. It solves "when is my bus coming and where is it now": it shows official live arrival times for any stop, every bus moving on the map, line routes and timetables, metro and tram frequencies, a trip planner, and a notification when your bus is a few stops away.

3. Setup and how to access the features
No login, setup or sample files are needed. On first launch, tap Continue (and optionally Not Now when asked about saved places).
- Outside Athens there are no stops near you. Tap the search bar and type "040" (a line) or "Syntagma" (a stop), or move the map to central Athens.
- Stop: tap any stop to see its lines and live arrival times.
- Line: tap a line to see its buses on the map. Tabs: Arrivals, Route, Timetable.
- Bus alert: on a line's Arrivals tab, tap the bell on an approaching bus. You are notified when it is a few stops away, also with the screen locked.
- Follow: tap the target button to keep the map on a bus.
- Metro/tram: tap a red M or tram station on the map.
- Trip planner: side menu → Plan a Trip.
- Favourites: tap the star on a stop or line.
- Live Activity: setting a bus alert or following a bus shows its minutes and stops left on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
- Widgets: long-press the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → BusMakro. Long-press the widget → Edit Widget to choose a stop's next buses or a line's timetable for a day.

4. External services
- OASA Telematics API (telematics.oasa.gr): official live arrivals, bus positions, routes and timetables. Public, no key.
- athens-transit-rt (open-source project; transit.haroldpoi.dev and angelospk.github.io/athens-transit-rt): live bus positions and line shapes built from OASA's public data.
- STASY public GTFS timetable (data.gov.gr), republished as rail.json on the app's website (tassosmak.github.io/BusMakro-Swift).
- Apple MapKit: maps and address search in the trip planner.
No accounts, payments, analytics, advertising or AI services.

5. Regional differences
The app works the same in every region. Its content is Athens public transport, so stops near you appear only in the Athens area; anywhere else, search and the map show the same Athens data.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material
Not applicable. The app shows public transport information that OASA and STASY publish openly for anyone to use. BusMakro is an independent app and does not claim to be affiliated with OASA or STASY.
