## English

**Promotional Text** (126/170)
Live OASA arrivals and every bus moving on the map. Metro, tram, trip planning and an alert when your bus is a few stops away.

**Description**
BusMakro shows when your bus is coming and where it is right now.

• Live arrivals: OASA's official times for every stop, with every bus moving on the map.
• Stops near you: opens on the stops around you. Move the map to explore any area.
• Lines: the route, its stops in order, the buses between them, and the timetable for any day.
• Bus alerts: tap the bell and get notified when your bus is a few stops away, even with the phone locked.
• Follow a bus: the map stays on it as it moves.
• Metro and tram: lines, stations and how often trains run.
• Trip planner: from where you are to any stop, station or address, by bus, metro and tram.
• Favourites: your stops and lines one tap away, and on a Home Screen widget.
• Offline: the last times you saw are saved, for when you're underground.
• Search in Greek or Latin letters.

No account, no ads, no tracking.

Data from OASA and STASY. BusMakro is an independent app, not affiliated with OASA or STASY.

## App Review Information

- **Sign-in required:** untick (the app has no accounts).
- **Contact:** your name, phone and email (only Apple sees these).
- **Notes:**

BusMakro needs no account. It shows live public transport in Athens, Greece (OASA buses and trolleys, STASY metro and tram), from OASA's public telematics API.

Outside Athens there are no nearby stops. To review from anywhere: tap the search bar and type a line such as 040 or a stop such as Syntagma, or move the map to central Athens.

Bus alerts: open a line, then tap the bell on an approaching bus. The app keeps using location ("While Using") after the screen locks only while an alert is on, to notify you when the bus is a few stops away.

## App Privacy (data types)

"Data Not Collected". Apple counts data as collected only when it's kept beyond answering the request. BusMakro sends your position to OASA only to find the stops near you, right then, with nothing that identifies you. It keeps no data of its own and has no analytics, ads or tracking.
