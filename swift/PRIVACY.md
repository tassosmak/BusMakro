# BusMakro Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 1 November 2026*

BusMakro is an unofficial app for Athens buses. It is not affiliated with OASA, OSY or STASY.

## The short version

BusMakro does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## Location

If you allow it, BusMakro uses your location to show the bus stops near you and to centre the map. Your location is used only on your device, while the app is open. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is never stored.

Nearby stops are found on the device. The app downloads bus times for the stop you open, not for where you are.

You can turn location access off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

## Notifications

If you set a bus alert ("notify me when the bus is N stops away"), BusMakro shows a local notification on your device. Nothing is sent to a push server.

## What stays on your device

Your favourite stops and lines, recent stops and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Network requests

To show live buses, the app requests public transit data from:

- **OASA Telematics** (telematics.oasa.gr): arrival times, lines, routes and stops
- **athens-transit-rt**: live bus positions and routes
- **OASA's website**: service alerts and strike notices
- **Apple Maps** (MapKit): the map itself, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

These requests contain only what is needed to fetch the data, such as a stop or line number. Like any internet request, they reveal your IP address to those servers. BusMakro adds no identifiers to them.

## Children

BusMakro collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
