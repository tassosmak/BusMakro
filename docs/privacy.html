BusMakro Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

BusMakro is an unofficial app for Athens buses, metro and tram. It is not affiliated with OASA, OSY or STASY.

The short version

BusMakro does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Location

If you allow it, BusMakro uses your location to show the stops near you, centre the map and plan trips from where you are. Your location is used only on your device, while the app is open. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and it is never stored.

Nearby stops are found on the device. The app downloads bus times for the stop or line you open, not for where you are.

You can turn location access off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Notifications and Live Activities

If you set a bus alert ("notify me when the bus is N stops away"), BusMakro shows a local notification and a Live Activity on your device. They are created on the device; nothing is sent to a push server.

What stays on your device

Your favourite stops and lines, their nicknames, recent stops and settings are saved only on your device. To let you find them from Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight, your favourite stops are added to the device's own search index. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Network requests

To show live data, the app requests public transit data from:

  • OASA Telematics (telematics.oasa.gr): arrival times, lines, routes, stops and timetables
  • athens-transit-rt: live bus positions and routes
  • OASA's website: service alerts and strike notices
  • Apple Maps (MapKit): the map and place search, under Apple's privacy policy

Metro and tram timetables are built into the app and need no connection.

These requests contain only what is needed to fetch the data, such as a stop or line number. Like any internet request, they reveal your IP address to those servers. BusMakro adds no identifiers to them.

Children

BusMakro collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes
