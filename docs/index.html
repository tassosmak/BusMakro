A look inside

Questions

Is it an official OASA app?

No. BusMakro is an independent app, not affiliated with OASA, OSY or STASY. It uses their public data.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

What does it do with my location?

It finds the stops near you and keeps an alert working with the phone locked. It never stores your location or sends it to us. Details in the privacy policy.

Why is a time marked with ~?

A green dot means the time is OASA's own. A ~ means BusMakro worked it out from where the bus is, when OASA had no time for it.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
